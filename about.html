Sri Raghavendra Typewriting Institute

Learn Typing Through Practice and Proper Training

We help students develop fast and accurate typing skills through regular practice and dedicated guidance, with training for Typewriting Lower, Junior, and Higher examinations.

What We Teach

Our training focuses on developing strong typing skills and preparing students for their typewriting examinations.

Fast Typing Training

Students practice regularly to improve their typing speed, accuracy, and confidence.

Typewriting Lower

Focused training and practice for students preparing for the Typewriting Lower examination.

Junior Examination

Proper typewriting practice and guidance for students preparing for the Junior examination.

Higher Examination

Continued training and practice for students preparing for the Typewriting Higher examination.

Our Approach

We believe that consistent practice and proper guidance are the foundation of good typewriting skills.

Regular Practice

Consistent practice helps students gradually improve their typing speed and accuracy.

Speed & Accuracy

Students are encouraged to develop both typing speed and accuracy instead of focusing on speed alone.

Dedicated Guidance

Students receive guidance throughout their training to help them improve their typewriting skills and prepare for examinations.
